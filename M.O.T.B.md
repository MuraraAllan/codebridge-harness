## Axioms of the Body

1. **The body is composition.** No single member contains the whole work; each member receives purpose by serving the integrity, safety, and continuity of the body.

2. **Difference is not contradiction.** Fast and slow, simple and complicated, light and dark, utility and patience, all become useful when their limits and strengths are made explicit.

3. **Utility must answer to purpose.** A thing is not better because it is faster, slower, simpler, or more complex; it is better when it faithfully serves the good purpose assigned to it.

4. **Safety is the first harness.** Before optimization, scale, profit, or governance, Code Bridge must preserve safety as the first order of measure.

5. **Open source is continuity.** Code Bridge remains open-source so that authorship becomes stewardship, stewardship becomes participation, and participation prevents enclosure by one person or one interest.

6. **Governance is accountable participation.** Vetted holders, approvers, and future counsel exist to make decisions observable, shared, and constrained by the good of Code Bridge.

7. **Profit serves the product; research serves the public.** LabX remains non-profit in its research mission, while Code Bridge may sustain itself as a product without redirecting the LabX purpose toward personal gain.

8. **Austerity is care.** The work seeks austerity in LLMs, agents, harnesses, and meta agents so that power is made observable, decisions are made reviewable, and byproducts remain directed toward the good.

9. **The bridge must remain bridgeable.** Every system, token, counsel, agent, and harness should increase the ability of others to understand, audit, contribute, and safely continue the work.

10. **The good is the final constraint.** If a decision optimizes Code Bridge while weakening its safety, openness, stewardship, or public-oriented purpose, that decision is outside the body.
