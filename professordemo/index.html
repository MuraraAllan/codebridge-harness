Archaeological Hull Architecture

Mycenaean Shipbuilding

Based on the Tragana pyxis (Pylos Museum) & Kynos naval sherd reconstructions.

1. Keel & Shell-First Construction

Planks joined edge-to-edge by pegged mortise-and-tenon joints (confirmed by Uluburun & Cape Gelidonya wrecks). No internal rib skeleton prior to planking.

2. Homeric Nomenclature (Ὅμηρος)

  • Στεῖρα (Steira): Stempost / cutwater at prow.
  • Τρόπις (Tropis): Keel running bow to stern.
  • Ζυγά (Zyga): Thwarts/crossbeams seating rowers.
  • Πηδάλια (Pedalia): Twin lateral steering oars.
  • Μεσόδμη (Mesodme): Wooden crutch/step for the mast.
  • Μιλτοπάρῃοι (Miltoparioi): "Red-cheeked" vermilion prow.

3. Complement & Dimensions

Length: ~28–31 m
Beam: ~3.8–4.2 m
Draft: ~0.75 m
Crew: 50 rowers + 2-4 officers
